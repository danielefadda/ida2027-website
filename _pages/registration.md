## Registration for IDA 2027

Registration is now open. Early registration is recommended to benefit from reduced rates.

## Important Notes

- **Authors of accepted papers** must register by the camera-ready deadline
- Registration is **non-refundable**
- Registration is only possible through the official registration website
- Student rate requires proof of enrollment (BSc / MSc / PhD)
- Formal invitation letters are sent only to accepted paper authors upon explicit request

## Free SIKS PhD Student Seats

A fixed number of free seats are available for full-registered SIKS PhD students on a first-come-first-served basis. Please contact the organizers for details.
